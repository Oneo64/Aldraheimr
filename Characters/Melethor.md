Melethôr is a mercenary moon elf from Endarassil.

## Appearance
Melethôr has smooth, light blue skin and straight purple hair down to the shoulders. His eyes are grey and pupilless, and his ears are long and pointed.

Melethör wears a dark aqua tunic, brown pants, a belt, and boots. When in combat or travelling, he wears a leather helmet and a suit of lamellar armour, and carries a fangtian ji as his primary weapon, along with a liuyuedao sheathed in a scabbard.

## Personality
Melethôr is calm and somewhat playful. Like his brother (Sasôl), he would sometimes engage in horsing around during free time, which could be seen as harmless mischief by other people. Melethör is reserved, but he isn't introverted at all, meaning that he will stay just as quiet, but doesn't care about how many people are around him.

## Abilities
### Acrobatics
Melethôr is proficient in acrobatics and stealth, evident when he can silently perch on a tree branch or hop between them without falling.

### Halberd
Melethôr is proficient with his halberd skills.

### Language
Melethôr speaks Ranûdan and Centrumic (English). However, he always speaks in an OSV word order, reflecting his mother tongue.
