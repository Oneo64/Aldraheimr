Säelrie is a druid healer who works in Vesesvala.

## Appearance
Säelrie has vibrant green pupilless eyes, pale skin, golden hair, and elongated earlobes. She wears a white cloak and a dark aqua tunic-dress, and a jade and malachite bead bracelet on her right arm.

Säelrie carries around a pine wood staff, tipped with a small amber orb.

## Personality
Säelrie is usually calm and patient, with the generosity to help others without asking. She has a warm outlook, blatantly caring for things she finds adorable. However, she is disorganised and has little sense of money saving, tending to spend more than she should on things like food or trivial things she finds interesting.

## Abilities
Being a druid, Säelrie can speak Druidic and Sylvan aside from her native language (Esrel). She has an easier time approaching animals and letting them know she's friendly. Not only that, she is immune to all poison and disease. She also knows about Furnalfa.

Many years ago, Säelrie travelled to Furnalfa to retrieve an ancient Ranûladaenic tablet stolen by evil fey.

Säelrie learned Centrumic (English) to talk to humans.

### Druidry
Säelrie practices druidry.

**Druidic powers:**
- Invoke small plants
- Talk to animals
- Heal wounds
- Cleanse liquid
- Shapeshift
    - Brown bear (combat)
    - Cat (stealth)
    - Elk (travel)
    - Raven (flight)
