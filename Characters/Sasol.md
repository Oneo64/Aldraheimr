Sasöl is a moon elf from Endarassil who works as a ranger, patrolling the forest outskirts of the elven city.

## Appearance
Sasöl has light blue skin and straight purple hair down to the shoulders. His eyes are grey and pupilless, and his ears are long and pointed. His height is 5'11".

Sasöl wears a dark aqua gambeson, brown pants, simple leather bracers, a belt, and a pair of boots. He also has a plain silver circlet, a quiver on his hip filled with obsidian-tipped arrows, and a longbow with a twisting pattern. When he is at the outskirts of Endarassil guarding, he wears the Rànûdan archer uniform.

## Personality
Sasöl is calm in his actions but playful in his curiosity, although he is very rarely seen doing goofy things. Introverted to the heart, he would purposefully return home late at night just so there would be fewer people.

One distinct habit Sasöl has is that he would tilt his head whenever he didn't understand something or whenever he saw something interesting.

## Abilities
### Archery and Acrobatics
Sasöl is quite good with his bow. He is proficient in acrobatics and stealth, evident when he can silently perch on a tree branch or hop between them without falling. This leads him to combine some of his abilities just to show off, such as balancing on a branch while hitting a nearby target right on without falling.

### Language
Sasöl speaks Ranûdan and Hendric (English). However, he always speaks in an OSV word order, reflecting his mother tongue.
