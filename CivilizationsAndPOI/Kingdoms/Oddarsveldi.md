Oddarsveldi is a major kingdom located in the northern region of Myrkjörðr.

## History
Oddarsveldi rose to power from a small village named Hrattvekr. After Oddr the First led a defensive force, defeating an army of invading trolls, he was made king.

## Social Hierarchy
The social hierarchy of Oddarsveldi is, from the highest to lowest: king, jarl, hersir, huskarl, free men, and slaves. It is feudal and family-based, but if a family is unable to continue its legacy (no other male heirs) or the family's heir abuses their power, the family can lose power in favour of another family.

### King
The King of Oddarsveldi has absolute power over the kingdom.

### Jarls
Jarls are greater nobles who live in a ring fortress and oversee their settlements, such as trade or wellbeing, and have power over them to make sure all goes well. They can rally troops, enforce laws, build new structures, and even carry out executions.

Jarls can assign new hersirs in the settlements they have control over, but both powers, including hersirs, cannot step down themselves and instead must arrange this with a higher authority.

### Hersirs
Hersirs have almost the same power as jarls, except for a few limitations. Firstly, their power is tied to their own settlement. Secondly, to condemn someone or to order an execution, they must arrange it through a thing (medieval gathering) with trusted free men.

### Huskarl
Huskarls are noble warriors who serve the king, a jarl, or a hersir. They are basically knights. If the king cannot find a suitable heir, he can choose a huskarl to crown.

### Free men
Free men are the middle class people of Oddarsveldi. Most people who live here are free men.

### Slaves
Most slaves are either prisoners of war or convicted criminals serving community service for the king, a jarl, or a hersir. Slaves and servants are NOT the same (servants are free men doing a job).

## Military
The Oddarish army has around two thousand warriors, but most of them live in their homes.

### Uniforms
Oddarish soldiers are required to bring their own weapons. However, royal guards are provided with a coppergate helmet, maille hauberk, and a spear.
