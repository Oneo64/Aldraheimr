After leaving the Mountains of Ranûdel in an act of rebellion, the elves' bodies began to change with the weather. Their hair and skin adopted hardy colours, and their ears began to droop like the dew on leaves. Cold elves have a natural resistance to extreme cold and bear excellent eyesight in bright places.
