After leaving the Mountains of Ranûdel in an act of rebellion, the elves' bodies began to change with the weather. Their hair and skin adopted hardy Moon elves were the first beings to ever walk on the lands. Bearing the most ethereal features, they were created by Ainassare as their children. Moon elves have a keen sense of balance, allowing them to perch on high places, and natural darkvision.
